**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is a branch of computer science focused on building systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. Instead of relying strictly on hardcoded, step-by-step instructions, AI systems learn from data to solve problems, recognize patterns, and make decisions.

Here is a quick breakdown of what it is and how it works:

---

## The Core Concept: How AI Thinks

Traditional software follows strict rules: *If X happens, do Y.*
AI works differently. It is fed massive amounts of data and discovers the rules on its own: *Here are 10,000 examples of X and Y. Figure out the pattern connecting them.*

This pattern-recognition ability allows AI to handle complex tasks like understanding human speech, driving autonomous vehicles, and predicting stock market trends.

---

## The Two Main Types of AI

* **Narrow AI (Weak AI):** Systems designed to handle a single, specific task exceptionally well. This is the AI we use today. Examples include facial recognition, Google Search algorithms, Spotify recommendations, and chess-playing bots.
* **General AI (Strong AI / AGI):** A hypothetical form of AI that possesses human-like cognitive abilities across all domains. An AGI could learn, reason, and apply knowledge to *any* problem, just like a human. True AGI does not yet exist.

---

## Key Technologies Driving AI

AI serves as an umbrella term for several distinct technologies:

| Technology | What It Does | Real-World Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Machine Learning (ML)** | Algorithms that automatically improve through experience and data. | Email spam filters. |
| **Deep Learning** | A subset of ML that uses multi-layered artificial neural networks to mimic the human brain. | Autonomous self-driving cars. |
| **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** | Tech that enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. | Voice assistants like Siri or Alexa. |
| **Generative AI** | Models trained to create entirely new content, such as text, images, code, or music. | ChatGPT, Midjourney, and Gemini. |

---

> **The Big Picture:** AI is not a single product or app—it is a foundational utility, much like electricity or the internet. It is rapidly transforming industries by automating repetitive work, analyzing massive datasets in seconds, and acting as a collaborative tool for human creativity.
